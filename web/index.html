Not sure? Try one of these:

  1. तुम समझाओ। You explain any topic aloud, in your own words.
  2. Samjhao सुनता है और ज़्यादा से ज़्यादा 3 छोटे सवाल पूछता है, उसी हिस्से पर जो छूट गया या गड़बड़ था। Samjhao listens and asks at most 3 short questions, about the part you skipped or mixed up. That is how it finds out what you really understood.
  3. अटक गए? Hint मिलेगा, जवाब नहीं। Stuck? You get a hint, not the answer.
  4. आख़िर में पूरा explanation, diagram के साथ। At the end, the full explanation with a diagram. You can jump to it at any time.
