How it works

  1. Say any topic, in Hindi, English, Punjabi, Telugu or Kannada, or a mix.
  2. Explain it aloud, as if to your younger brother or sister.
  3. Samjhao asks one short question about the part you skipped. It does not give the answer.
  4. See which points you explained on your own.

No login. Nothing is stored after a session.
